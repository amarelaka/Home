Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 8, 2026

Welcome to Amar Elaka (“we,” “our,” or “us”). Amar Elaka provides a website and mobile application intended to help people access local information and community services. We respect your privacy and explain below what information may be handled when you use our services and how it may be used.

Please note: The information described in this policy depends on the features you use and the information you choose to provide. Please do not submit passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information unless a feature specifically requires it and you understand why.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect or receive:

  • Account information: such as your name, email address, and information associated with your account.
  • Information you provide: details, messages, reviews, requests, or other information you choose to submit through the website or application.
  • Community-service information: information you voluntarily provide for features such as blood-donor listings or emergency-contact information.
  • Location information: only where a feature requires it and, where applicable, after you grant permission through your device.
  • Technical information: limited device, browser, diagnostic, and usage information that may be processed to operate, maintain, and secure the services.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage user accounts.
  • To provide local information and community features.
  • To operate features related to blood donors and emergency contacts.
  • To respond to questions, feedback, and support requests.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the website and application.
  • To help protect users, prevent misuse, and maintain service security.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Information Visibility and Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information may be shared in these limited circumstances:

  • With other users: information you submit to a community feature may be visible to other users when that feature is designed to display it. Please review the information you submit before publishing it.
  • With service providers: trusted providers may process information on our behalf to support hosting, authentication, databases, security, or other technical services.
  • For legal or safety reasons: information may be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or the security of the services.

4. Third-Party Services

Some parts of Amar Elaka may use third-party services, which may include Google Firebase for services such as authentication, database, hosting, or related application functions. The use of these services may involve processing information under the providers’ own privacy terms and policies.

Third-party websites or services linked from Amar Elaka are governed by their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies before providing information to them.

5. Location and Device Permissions

Some features may request access to device permissions, such as location. You can manage or withdraw permissions through your device settings. If you deny a permission, the related feature may not work as intended.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no website, application, or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion Requests

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the services, meet legitimate operational needs, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided. We may need to verify your request before acting on it. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

Amar Elaka is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental or guardian consent is required. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the request.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, practices, or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date. Please review this page periodically.
